

- The sol grid is the core of the game. I envision many things: abilities are chips that go in there. Batteries will go in there to expand your max sol. Generators will go in there to increase sol/time. You might have chips to extend your survivability in night or day that cost sol/time. (So you balance with your generators and either have a surplus or deficit, which creates different dynamics of play, depending on where you need to go.) There are modifiers, such as boosts, passive player boosts like HP/armor/etc. Then there are combos and I imagine simple adjacency things that already require some theorycrafting but also "programming" such as having mods: [for each] [ability] [increase damage] [sol cone], which would apply the "increase damage" to sol cone N times, where N is your number of abilities.

- Upgrading sol unit allows you to traverse further from the habitable zone. Going into either day/night without a sol unit will result in damage over time. Your sol unit will have two properties: day_survivability and night_survivability. To the extent that you're above your day/night survivability, you will take damage over time.

- There is an automation loop inspired by stardew valley. This definitely will involve deplying solar panels at some point. I imagine batteries (which are a resource you want for your suit) will allow you to create drones that can go into the nightside to harvest things there.

- The dayside is occupied by an AI solar grid which is in theory infrastructure but turns out to have motives of its own. The dayside represents the extreme of progress The nightside is occupied by the luddites which seem to be evil but turns out to be normal with a bad faction amongst them. The nightside represents the extreme of fear. The habitable zone is occupied by civilization and represents the human condition: sandwiched between light and dark, always on the line of these two, needing both to survive.

- All fuel on the planet has been consumed. The only energy is from solar or from off-planet uranium. Lighthouses are powered by off-planet uranium. The player can obtain very precious uranium generators that slot into the sol grid and provide passive energy regen.

- The dayside and nightside definitely have primary resources that can be found there, and that are needed on the other side.

- There should be a worldmap - ideally traversable by foot or by craft to give an open world feel.

- There are three progression systems: (1) the main one is your sol unit / sol grid. (2) the second one is the automation loop. (3) you also level up and get skill points in passives and allow holding higher level gear/mods, just to make it worth it to engage in combat rather than skating past foes.

- You harvest something nightside by dungeon crawling that you trade to the dayside for your abilities. That "trade" interface should only have these abilities and upgraded versions of those abilities. This is the "skill tree" which should be the most exciting part of the early game. It should be clear that you can unlock this in a linear fashion by progressing through the dungeon crawling side of the game.

- Modifiers are random drops which can dramatically change your efficacy but not your ability tree.

Very likely endgame automation will be getting us uranium from off planet, question is whether we ourselves can go there yet or not.

- QUESTION: What is the ultimate enemy we need to defeat? Starts by seeming like the luddites ends up being...?

- BIGGEST QUESTION 1: Exactly what is the automation and gameplay loop like? To what extent do we synchronize progress between them? How do we punctuate the two so that (a) you're compelled to do both, (b) you feel like you're free to choose which to focus on, and (c) it feels natural/good to go back and forth between them. Stardew is the shining example here where you can choose to farm a little and dungeon crawl a little every day, focus just on one, or focus just on the other. How do we get something that feels as good here?  [ I guess resource gathering should start out as a manual process? ]

- BIGGEST QUESTION 2: What is the automation loop like. How does it start, how does it progress, what does it give you.
