# Theme: Meridian City

## Setting
The urban heart of civilization on Erith — a Terminator Zone city-state governed by the Meridian Council. This is a real city with districts, markets, workshops, archives, and civic buildings. After weeks on the dark frontier, arriving here is intoxicating — light, noise, people, commerce. The architecture reflects a society that has built and rebuilt over generations under permanent twilight, blending industrial practicality with civic pride.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for a lived-in sci-fi city with a strong sense of community and permanence. Not sleek futuristic chrome — this is a working city with layers of construction, repair, and adaptation. Think a blend of industrial-era civic architecture with improvised solar-punk technology. Warm interior lighting spills from windows. Streets are busy-feeling even when empty.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Paved streets with fitted stone blocks (not asphalt — stone is more available). Covered market walkways with wooden planking. Civic buildings have polished stone floors with geometric inlay patterns. Workshop districts have oil-stained concrete. Some areas have decorative mosaic tiles in public squares.
- **Walls:** Substantial stone construction with arched windows and doorframes. Metal shutters and awnings over shopfronts. Civic buildings have carved facades and Council insignia. Workshop areas have corrugated metal and ventilation ducts. Residential areas have plastered walls with painted trim in muted colors.
- **Lighting:** Warm and abundant compared to the frontier. Bioluminescent panel strips along building facades (cultivated organisms, not electric). Solar-charged lamps at intersections. Interior glow from workshops and shops. The overall feel is golden-warm twilight, a stark contrast to the cold blue of the perimeter.
- **Props/Details:** Market stalls with displayed goods, hanging signs for shops, notice boards with Council postings, planter boxes with twilight-adapted vegetation, benches, fountain features (water is precious and displayed as civic pride), tool carts, cargo being moved, architectural details like carved lintels and ironwork.
- **Atmosphere:** Alive. Hopeful. This is what the Lightkeepers are protecting. The city has problems — political tension, resource anxiety, the Array's shadow — but it's fundamentally a place where people live, work, trade, and plan for the future. The tiles should feel warm and grounded, a welcome refuge.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Warm sandstone, amber, architectural gray
- Accent: Bioluminescent soft green/gold (lighting panels), deep red (Council banners), copper (metalwork)
- Shadows: Warm brown, not cold — interior shadows feel sheltered, not threatening

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (paved stone, polished interior, wooden planking, market cobblestone)
- Wall tiles (stone facades, shop fronts, civic architecture, residential walls)
- Door tiles (arched doorways, shop entrances, civic double doors)
- Stair tiles (stone steps, ramps)
- Special tiles (market stalls, notice boards, fountains, planter boxes, bioluminescent panels)
