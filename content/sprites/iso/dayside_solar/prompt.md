# Theme: Dayside Solar Fields

## Setting
The scorching light side of a tidally locked planet, where an eternal sun beats down on vast solar harvesting arrays operated by the Array — an autonomous AI civilization. The player visits border terminals at the edge of human survivability, where the heat shimmers and the light is blinding. This is where humanity trades with the machines: bring minerals, receive energy and technology.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for an alien, sun-blasted industrial landscape. Everything is designed for maximum solar exposure — reflective surfaces, geometric precision, crystalline structures. No organic life survives here. The architecture is machine-built: perfect angles, optimal spacing, no wasted material. Beautiful in an inhuman, mathematical way.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Fused glass plains (sand melted by eternal sun), polished metal plating at terminals, geometric grid patterns etched into heat-resistant composite. The ground itself shimmers with heat distortion. Crystalline mineral deposits sparkle in the relentless light. Some areas have solar collection panels embedded flush in the ground.
- **Walls:** Reflective composite panels angled to redirect sunlight. Terminal structures are modular — machine-assembled with perfect tolerances. Solar array pylons rise in mathematically spaced grids. Heat shields create shaded corridors for human visitors. The Array's structures are elegant but alien — no doors in the human sense, just apertures that open when approached.
- **Lighting:** Overwhelming. The dominant light source is the sun — eternal, unblinking, casting hard shadows with no diffusion. Everything is either blazing bright or in knife-edge shadow. The solar terminals have UV-filtering canopies that cast the trading areas in a slightly cooler amber. Array structures glow faintly with internal processing — circuits visible through translucent panels.
- **Props/Details:** Solar harvester drones (compact, geometric), mineral trading terminals (sleek interfaces), energy cell dispensers, heat warning indicators, radiation gauges, Array communication nodes (geometric shapes that pulse with data), heat shimmer effects, player-deployed solar panels (cruder and more human-looking than Array tech).
- **Atmosphere:** Awe and unease. The Dayside is magnificent and hostile. The Array's infrastructure extends to the horizon in perfect mathematical patterns. Standing here, you understand the scale of what humanity is dealing with — a machine intelligence that thinks in solar panels and optimization curves. The trade is mutually beneficial. The power imbalance is obvious.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Blazing white, chrome silver, sun-bleached gold
- Accent: Circuit blue (Array processing glow), solar amber (filtered light), crystalline prismatic (mineral deposits)
- Shadows: Hard black with no gradation — the sun allows no half-measures

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (fused glass, metal plating, solar panels, crystalline ground)
- Wall tiles (reflective panels, heat shields, Array structures)
- Door tiles (Array apertures, terminal access points)
- Special tiles (trading terminals, harvester pads, energy cells, Array communication nodes)
