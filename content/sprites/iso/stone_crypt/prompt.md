# Theme: Stone Crypt

## Setting
Ancient underground burial chambers and tunnels beneath the frontier outpost. These crypts predate the current settlement — carved from bedrock by earlier colonists or perhaps by even older hands. The air is cold and still. Bones rest in alcoves. The walls weep moisture. This is where the dead were laid before the outpost was built on top of them.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for a classic dungeon crypt — rough-hewn stone corridors, arched ceilings (implied by shadow), burial niches, and crumbling masonry. The style should evoke deep underground spaces that haven't been maintained in generations. Eerie but not cartoonish — grounded and atmospheric.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Rough-cut stone slabs, some cracked or shifted. Mortar visible between flagstones. Dust and fine gravel accumulate in corners. Occasional puddles of condensation or slow seepage. Some areas have crumbled to reveal raw bedrock beneath.
- **Walls:** Stacked stone blocks, some large and precisely fitted (older construction), some smaller and cruder (later repairs). Niches and alcoves carved into walls for burial urns. Iron brackets where torches once hung, now rusted and empty. Mineral deposits streak the walls in pale veins.
- **Lighting:** Near-total darkness. The only illumination comes from the player's Sol Unit — a harsh, directional glow that casts long shadows. Faint phosphorescent lichen on some walls provides the slightest ambient glow in blue-green.
- **Props/Details:** Crumbled bone fragments, toppled urns, ancient carved symbols worn smooth by time, collapsed sections where ceiling stones have fallen, wooden support beams dry-rotted and splintering, corroded iron chains and hooks, cobwebs thick as curtains.
- **Atmosphere:** Oppressive silence. The weight of stone above. A place of rest disturbed by creatures that have moved in — skeletons animated by unknown forces, things that scuttle in the dark. The crypt is a reminder that this world has layers of history buried beneath the present.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Cold gray stone, charcoal, slate blue
- Accent: Bone white, rust orange (corroded metal), faint blue-green (phosphorescent lichen)
- Shadows: Near-black with blue undertones, suggesting deep underground cold

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (flagstone, cracked stone, rubble)
- Wall tiles (stacked stone blocks, carved niches)
- Door tiles (iron-banded wooden doors, locked gates)
- Stair tiles (carved stone steps, up/down)
- Special tiles (burial alcoves, water features, collapsed sections)
