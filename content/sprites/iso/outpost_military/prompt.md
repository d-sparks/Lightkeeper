# Theme: Outpost Military Base

## Setting
A fortified frontier outpost on the edge of the habitable Terminator Zone of a tidally locked planet. This is Outpost Balor — a hardened military station where Lightkeepers (frontier engineers/soldiers) are deployed. The outpost sits at the boundary between the twilight civilization and the permanent darkness of the Nightside.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for a rugged, utilitarian military compound. The architecture is functional, not decorative — prefabricated metal walls, reinforced concrete, modular structures bolted together over decades of use. Think a forward operating base crossed with an industrial maintenance depot.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Heavy-duty metal grating, worn concrete slabs with welded seams, non-slip diamond plate in high-traffic areas. Scuff marks and boot prints visible. Occasional drainage grates.
- **Walls:** Riveted steel panels, sandbagged reinforcements, blast-resistant composite. Some walls have conduit runs and cable bundles bolted to the outside. Faded military markings and stenciled room numbers.
- **Lighting:** Harsh overhead fluorescent strips, some flickering or broken. Emergency lighting strips along baseboards in red/amber. The light is cold and institutional.
- **Props/Details:** Supply crates stacked against walls, weapon racks, tool lockers, fire extinguishers, first aid stations, comms equipment with blinking LEDs, status boards with duty rosters, coffee-stained desks, folding chairs.
- **Atmosphere:** Tense, lived-in, battle-worn. This place has been patched and repaired many times. Welding scars on walls, replaced panels that don't quite match, temporary fixes that became permanent. The people here are under constant threat and the environment shows it.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Military olive drab, gunmetal gray, concrete beige
- Accent: Warning yellow on hazard stripes, red emergency lighting, faded blue stenciling
- Shadows: Deep blue-gray, suggesting cold from the Nightside seeping in

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (stone/metal variants)
- Wall tiles (reinforced panels, corners, junctions)
- Door tiles (blast doors, standard doors)
- Stair tiles (up/down)
- Special tiles (charging stations, workbenches, locked gates)
