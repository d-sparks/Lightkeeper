# Theme: Dark Perimeter

## Setting
The borderlands between the habitable Terminator Zone and the permanent Nightside of a tidally locked planet. This is the frontier — where the last Lighthouse light fades and true darkness begins. Fortified gates, patrol routes, watchtower foundations, and the scarred ground of decades of skirmishes with Nightside creatures and Luddite raiders. The temperature drops sharply. The wind carries sounds from the dark.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for a militarized frontier border zone transitioning into hostile wilderness. On one side, the last remnants of civilization — concrete barriers, chain-link fencing, floodlight pylons. On the other, raw Nightside terrain — frozen mud, dark rock, creeping frost. The tiles should convey a tense boundary between safety and the unknown.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Packed earth and gravel (patrol paths), cracked concrete (old fortification pads), frozen mud with boot prints, exposed bedrock with ice crystals forming in cracks. The ground transitions from maintained to wild across the zone.
- **Walls:** Concrete blast barriers stained with weather and combat. Chain-link topped with razor wire. Sandbag emplacements. Natural rock formations used as defensive positions. Ice-encrusted boulders at the Nightside edge.
- **Lighting:** Dim and directional. Floodlights (some broken) cast harsh cones. Beyond their reach, the darkness is absolute. Frost glitters at the edge of light. The sky is a permanent deep twilight — no sun, no stars, just a gradient from dark gray to black.
- **Props/Details:** Broken floodlight pylons, spent shell casings, razor wire tangles, abandoned guard posts, warning signs ("NIGHTSIDE BOUNDARY - AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY"), frozen footprints leading into the dark, claw marks on barriers, bloodstains on concrete.
- **Atmosphere:** The threshold. Behind you is everything you know. Ahead is lethal cold, alien predators, and the Unbounded. Every patrol out here is a gamble. The perimeter shows the cost — patched barriers, replaced sections, memorials scratched into concrete by soldiers who lost friends here.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Dark earth brown, frost white, gunmetal gray
- Accent: Warning orange (hazard signs), ice blue (frost), dried rust red (old bloodstains)
- Shadows: Deep black bleeding in from the Nightside, cold blue-purple at the edges

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (packed earth, concrete, frozen ground, rocky terrain)
- Wall tiles (blast barriers, chain-link, natural rock, ice formations)
- Door/gate tiles (fortified gates, checkpoint barriers)
- Stair tiles (terrain elevation changes, ladder access points)
- Special tiles (floodlight positions, guard posts, ravine edges)
