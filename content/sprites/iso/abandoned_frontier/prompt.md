# Theme: Abandoned Frontier

## Setting
Derelict structures scattered across the border of the habitable zone — old watchtowers, survey stations, collapsed relay outposts, and the Dead Road that once connected settlements now lost to the dark. These places were abandoned when the Nightside crept forward or when the resources to maintain them ran out. Nature and cold have been reclaiming them. Luddite raiders sometimes shelter here. The structures are layered with history — each one tells a story of retreat.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for ruined, weather-beaten frontier architecture slowly being consumed by the environment. The original construction was utilitarian — similar to outpost architecture but older, cruder, and now falling apart. Ice, frost, dark-adapted vegetation, and structural collapse have transformed these buildings into something between shelter and hazard.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Cracked and heaved concrete where frost has worked its way underneath. Stone foundations exposed where floor material has been scavenged. Packed earth and gravel where floors have collapsed entirely. Frost patterns on every surface. Dead vegetation — dried vines, brittle moss — in cracks and seams. Salvage debris scattered underfoot.
- **Walls:** Partially collapsed stone and concrete walls. Gaps where materials have been removed for reuse elsewhere. Exposed rebar and structural beams, rusted and bent. Makeshift repairs by past occupants — scavenged sheet metal, stacked stones, tarps that have long since frozen solid. Some walls have Luddite markings — territorial symbols scratched or painted.
- **Lighting:** The twilight of the Terminator Zone — dim, flat, without strong direction. The sky is a permanent overcast gray. No artificial light remains. The Sol Unit provides the only focused illumination, revealing details that daylight would miss — claw marks on walls, frozen bootprints, supply caches hidden in rubble.
- **Props/Details:** Toppled watchtower sections, collapsed roof beams, old survey equipment (broken theodolites, snapped measuring poles), salvage yard items (stripped machinery, sorted scrap metal, repair benches), Luddite camp remnants (fire pits, animal bones, rough bedding), supply caches sealed in weatherproof containers, road markers pointing to settlements that no longer exist.
- **Atmosphere:** Melancholy and exposed. Every ruin here was someone's workplace, someone's post. The Dead Road was once a busy supply route. The watchtowers once had sentries who scanned the dark for threats. Now the wind blows through broken walls and the cold creeps in. But these ruins aren't empty — scavengers, raiders, and dark-side creatures all find shelter here, making every ruin a potential ambush.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Weathered gray concrete, frost white, dried earth brown
- Accent: Rust orange (corroded metal), frost blue (ice formations), faded military green (old equipment)
- Shadows: Cold blue-gray, suggesting the encroaching Nightside

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (cracked concrete, exposed foundation, packed earth, frost-covered stone)
- Wall tiles (partially collapsed walls, makeshift repairs, exposed structure)
- Door tiles (broken doorframes, collapsed passages, barricaded entrances)
- Stair tiles (crumbling steps, ladder remains, terrain scrambles)
- Special tiles (salvage piles, old equipment, road markers, watchtower bases, camp remnants)
