# Theme: Natural Cave

## Setting
Unworked cave systems in the rock beneath the Terminator Zone, formed by geological forces over millennia. These are not the constructed crypts — they're raw, natural caverns with dripping water, mineral formations, and narrow passages. Some have been used as caches or hideouts. Others contain strange signals emanating from deep below. The caves connect the surface world to deeper, stranger places.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for organic, rough-hewn cave environments. Irregular shapes, natural rock formations, water features, and the claustrophobic feeling of being deep underground in spaces not made for humans. The caves should feel geologically real — stalactites, flowstone, mineral veins, underground streams.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Uneven natural rock, sometimes wet and slippery. Flowstone formations creating smooth, rippled surfaces. Gravel beds from water erosion. Mud patches near water. Crystal formations poking through the floor in some areas — clear quartz, dark umbrasite, pale calcite.
- **Walls:** Rough, undulating cave walls with natural texture. Stalactites hanging from above (visible in top of wall tiles). Mineral veins running through the rock — silver, copper, and the dark crystalline umbrasite that the Array craves. Water seepage creating dark streaks and mineral deposits. Some walls have been scratched or marked by previous explorers.
- **Lighting:** Darkness broken only by the player's Sol Unit and natural features. Some caves have faint bioluminescent moss or fungal growths — pale blue-green pinpoints in the dark. Underground water reflects and scatters light beautifully. Mineral crystals catch the Sol Unit's beam and scatter it in unexpected directions.
- **Props/Details:** Fallen stalactites, underground pools, narrow fissures, bat-like creatures clinging to ceilings, discarded explorer equipment (old lanterns, pitons, frayed rope), cached supply boxes hidden in alcoves, strange resonating crystals that hum when approached, fossilized remains of ancient organisms embedded in the rock.
- **Atmosphere:** Primal and untouched. The caves don't care about human politics or machine intelligence. They existed before colonization and will exist long after. The sound of dripping water echoes. The air is cold and mineral-scented. There's a sense that these passages go much deeper than anyone has explored — and that something down there is listening.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Raw stone gray-brown, wet dark gray, earth tones
- Accent: Crystal blue-white (mineral formations), bioluminescent blue-green (moss), warm amber (reflected Sol Unit light)
- Shadows: Deep black in crevices, blue-black in water features

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (rough rock, wet stone, gravel, flowstone, crystal formations)
- Wall tiles (natural cave walls, stalactite-heavy sections, mineral veins)
- Door tiles (narrow passages, squeeze points, collapsed sections)
- Stair tiles (natural rock steps, rope-assisted descents)
- Special tiles (underground pools, crystal clusters, cache hiding spots, resonance points)
