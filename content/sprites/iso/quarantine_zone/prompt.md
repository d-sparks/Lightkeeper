# Theme: Quarantine Zone

## Setting
Sealed-off sections of the outpost and underground areas that have been locked down due to contamination, structural danger, or creature incursion. These zones were once functional spaces — barracks, storage, utility corridors — but something went wrong. Now they're behind blast doors with warning signs, patrolled by whatever got in. The quarantine is both protection and prison — keeping the danger in, keeping the people out.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for corrupted, hazardous versions of familiar interior spaces. Take the outpost military aesthetic and layer damage, contamination, and neglect on top. Warning tape, hazmat markings, emergency lighting, and the visible signs of whatever caused the lockdown — structural damage, biological growth, electrical failure, or creature nesting.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** The original floor is visible but damaged — metal grating bent and torn, concrete cracked by impact or growth. Dark stains of unknown origin. Puddles of condensation or leaked coolant. Quarantine barrier tape on the ground marking safe/unsafe zones. Some areas have biological contamination — dark fungal growth spreading across surfaces, or crystalline deposits from Nightside mineral intrusion.
- **Walls:** Standard outpost construction visibly failing — panels buckled, wiring exposed and sparking, ventilation ducts torn open from the inside. Quarantine seals (heavy plastic sheeting, duct-taped and heat-sealed) cover some doorways. Warning placards: biohazard symbols, radiation warnings, "DO NOT ENTER" in stenciled red. Some walls have claw marks or impact damage from whatever caused the lockdown.
- **Lighting:** Emergency only. The main power has been cut or failed. Red emergency strips cast everything in an ominous crimson wash. Some sections have no light at all — the emergency system has been destroyed. Sparking exposed wires provide intermittent flashes. The player's Sol Unit cuts through the red with its own blue-white beam, creating unsettling color contrasts.
- **Props/Details:** Overturned furniture, scattered medical equipment, abandoned hazmat gear, sealed sample containers, quarantine barrier posts with warning tape, dead monitoring equipment, nest materials (for creature-infested zones), burst pipes, chemical spills with corrosion patterns, locked containment cells, emergency supply caches left for quarantine workers who never came back.
- **Atmosphere:** Dread and containment failure. Every quarantine zone tells a story of something going wrong and someone making the hard call to seal the doors. The question is always: what's still in there? The zones feel like the outpost's dark secret — the spaces behind the locked doors that nobody talks about but everyone knows exist.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Hazmat yellow, emergency red, damaged gray metal
- Accent: Contamination dark green/purple (biological growth), spark white (electrical), warning black/yellow stripes
- Shadows: Deep crimson (from emergency lighting), absolute black in dead zones

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (damaged metal, contaminated concrete, biological growth, chemical spills)
- Wall tiles (buckled panels, exposed wiring, quarantine seals, warning-marked surfaces)
- Door tiles (blast doors, quarantine barriers, sealed hatches)
- Stair tiles (damaged access ways, emergency ladders)
- Special tiles (containment cells, monitoring stations, hazmat equipment, nest formations)
