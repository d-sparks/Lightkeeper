# Theme: Homestead Residential

## Setting
Personal living spaces in the frontier settlements and Meridian city — modest homes, apartments, and the homestead lots where settlers build small-scale operations. These interiors are warm, personal, and human-scaled. After the military functionality of the outpost and the grandeur of the city, these spaces are intimate — a bed, a workbench, a garden plot, personal belongings. This is what people are fighting to protect.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for cozy, lived-in residential interiors and small garden plots. The aesthetic is frontier homesteading — practical but personalized. Handmade furniture, cultivated plants, personal effects, and the small comforts that make a dwelling a home. Not luxurious, but cared for.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Wooden plank floors, some with woven rugs. Kitchen areas have stone tile. Garden plots have rich dark soil with planted rows. Workshop corners have stained concrete or bare stone. Doorsteps have welcome mats. The floors creak and have character — knots, grain patterns, repair patches.
- **Walls:** Plastered interior walls in warm tones — cream, pale yellow, soft terracotta. Some walls have shelving with books, jars, and keepsakes. Kitchen walls have utensil hooks and spice racks. Workshop walls have pegboard with tools. Windows (implied by light patterns) look out onto twilight.
- **Lighting:** Warm and soft. Bioluminescent panel strips (domesticated organisms) cast a gentle golden glow. Table lamps with fabric shades. Kitchen areas are brighter. Bedrooms are dimmer. Garden plots have grow-lights (small solar-charged units). The overall feeling is firelight-warm, a sanctuary from the cold outside.
- **Props/Details:** Beds with quilted covers, dining tables with chairs, cooking surfaces with pots, water filtration units, seed pots with sprouting plants, personal logs/journals, framed photos or drawings, children's toys (in some units), tool sets, storage trunks, notice boards with community postings, garden tools (trowels, watering cans, seed packets).
- **Atmosphere:** Safety and normalcy. This is the human scale of the world — while Lightkeepers fight in the dark and politicians argue in the Council, people here grow food, raise children, repair tools, and plan for next season. The homestead is a reminder of what's at stake. It should feel warm, personal, and worth protecting.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Warm wood brown, cream plaster, garden earth
- Accent: Plant green (cultivated vegetation), fabric colors (quilt patterns, rug weaves), copper (kitchen and tools)
- Shadows: Warm brown, golden edges — interior shadows feel safe, not ominous

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (wooden planks, kitchen stone, garden soil, workshop concrete)
- Wall tiles (plastered interior, shelving, kitchen fittings, window walls)
- Door tiles (wooden interior doors, garden gates)
- Special tiles (garden plots, seed pots, workbenches, notice boards, personal log stations)
