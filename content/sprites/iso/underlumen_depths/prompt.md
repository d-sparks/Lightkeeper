# Theme: Underlumen Depths

## Setting
The deep Nightside — far beyond any Lighthouse's reach — where ancient structures predate human colonization by millennia. These ruins belong to the Underlumen, a planet-spanning fungal-crystalline intelligence that has been dormant since the Lighthouse network was built to suppress it. The structures are geometric, alien, and unsettling. They respond to light in unexpected ways. Something vast and patient is waking up down here.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for alien, pre-human architecture deep underground. The design language is completely different from anything human-built — organic geometry, crystalline structures, surfaces that seem to grow rather than be constructed. Think the intersection of geological formation and intentional design — structures that could be natural if they weren't so perfectly patterned. The Underlumen is biological and mineral simultaneously.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Smooth, dark crystalline surfaces with geometric patterns that look grown rather than carved. Hexagonal or fractal tiling in deep purple-black stone. Veins of bioluminescent material running through the floor like a circulatory system — faint, pulsing, responsive to the player's Sol Unit. Some areas have a thin layer of crystalline growth — like frost, but structured, forming repeating patterns.
- **Walls:** Towering geometric surfaces with carved (or grown) patterns — spirals, fractals, wave functions. The material is unlike anything on the surface — dark, semi-translucent, with internal luminescence that shifts when observed. Crystalline formations emerge from walls like frozen coral. Some walls have apertures that aren't quite doors — organic openings that suggest the structure was designed for inhabitants of a very different shape.
- **Lighting:** The Underlumen glows. Not brightly — a deep, slow pulse of violet, teal, and amber that moves through the crystalline substrate like a heartbeat. The player's Sol Unit interacts with this glow — some patterns brighten in response, others dim. The lighting feels alive. Certain chambers become fully illuminated when the Sol Unit hits specific wavelengths, revealing data encoded in the walls: star charts, energy diagrams, what might be warnings.
- **Props/Details:** Crystalline pillars that resonate with harmonic tones when approached. Data surfaces — walls that display encoded information when lit correctly. Fungal networks spreading along floors and walls — part of the Underlumen's communication system, thin filaments of bioluminescent mycelium. Mineral deposits of umbrasite — the crystallized substrate the Array desperately wants. Dormant mechanisms — structures that clearly have a purpose but haven't been activated in millennia.
- **Atmosphere:** Awe and existential unease. This place is older than humanity's presence on the planet. It is beautiful in a way that has nothing to do with human aesthetics. The Underlumen is not hostile — but it is vast, ancient, and utterly alien. Standing in these chambers, you understand that humanity is a recent, small presence on a world with its own deep intelligence. The hum you feel through the floor is the planet thinking.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Deep crystalline black-purple, dark teal, obsidian
- Accent: Bioluminescent violet (Underlumen pulse), warm amber (reactive glow), pale teal (mycelium networks), umbrasite prismatic dark
- Shadows: Deep indigo, almost cosmic — the darkness here isn't empty, it's full

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (crystalline geometric, pulsing vein patterns, mycelium-covered, umbrasite deposits)
- Wall tiles (alien geometric surfaces, crystalline formations, data walls, organic apertures)
- Door tiles (Underlumen apertures, resonance-activated passages)
- Stair tiles (crystalline step formations, descent into deeper chambers)
- Special tiles (resonance points, data surfaces, dormant mechanisms, umbrasite nodes)
