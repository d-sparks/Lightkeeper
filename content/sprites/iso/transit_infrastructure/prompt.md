# Theme: Transit Infrastructure

## Setting
The rail and transit network connecting settlements along the Terminator Zone. Train stations, relay junctions, signal boxes, and the tracks that thread between cities through the twilight. This infrastructure is the lifeline of civilization — when it works. Some sections are well-maintained; others have been abandoned as the frontier has shifted. Junction boxes hum with routing power. Platforms echo with the memory of busier times.

## Visual Direction
Isometric tile art for industrial transit architecture — train platforms, signal corridors, relay stations, and junction rooms. The style is heavy-industrial with a transit-specific character: tiled platform edges, overhead gantries, signal equipment, track beds. A mix of maintained and deteriorating sections, reflecting a transit system under strain.

## Key Visual Elements
- **Flooring:** Platform tiles — hard-wearing ceramic in institutional patterns (checkerboard, herringbone) with yellow safety lines along platform edges. Track beds with wooden sleepers and iron rails. Junction rooms have heavy steel grating over cable runs. Corridors have poured concrete with expansion joints. Some areas have puddles where drainage has failed.
- **Walls:** Tiled walls in station areas (glazed ceramic, easy to clean, some cracked or missing). Signal rooms have exposed cable racks and junction boxes. Corrugated metal in utility areas. Concrete tunnel walls in track sections, sometimes with moss growth near ventilation shafts.
- **Lighting:** Fluorescent strip lighting along platforms (some buzzing, some dead). Signal lights — red, green, amber — at junction points. Emergency lighting in tunnels. The overall light is industrial-white with warm amber accents from signal equipment. Deeper tunnel sections are dark, lit only by the player's Sol Unit and distant signal lamps.
- **Props/Details:** Departure boards (some functional, some blank), ticket barriers, signal control panels with levers and dials, overhead power cables, ventilation fans, platform benches, schedule postings yellowed with age, maintenance carts on tracks, tool cabinets, fire hose stations, junction boxes trailing thick cables.
- **Atmosphere:** The hum of infrastructure. The transit system was built to connect communities, and it still does — barely. Some stations are busy and well-lit. Others have been mothballed, their platforms silent, their signals dark. The junction rooms feel like the nervous system of civilization — small rooms full of critical equipment, maintained by people who understand that if the trains stop, the settlements die.

## Color Palette
- Dominant: Institutional cream/white tile, concrete gray, iron black (tracks/grating)
- Accent: Signal red/green/amber, safety yellow (platform edges), copper (old wiring)
- Shadows: Tunnel black, industrial blue-gray

## Tile Types Needed
- Floor tiles (platform ceramic, track bed, steel grating, concrete corridor)
- Wall tiles (glazed tile, cable racks, tunnel concrete, corrugated metal)
- Door tiles (platform gates, maintenance access, transit barriers)
- Stair tiles (platform steps, tunnel access)
- Special tiles (signal equipment, junction boxes, departure boards, track switches)
