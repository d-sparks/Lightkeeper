Progression thoughts:
- Sol unit -- class
    - Modules -- active abilities, progression based
        - Offensive
            - Cone attack (get this first)
            - Focus attack
        - Defensive
            - Shield
        - Utility
            - Hover
            - Spotlight (for puzzles)
    - Mods -- passive abilities, rolls + endgame content
        - Damage
        - Survivability (life leach, evasion, armor, DR, health)
        - Heal efficacy
- Weapon -- Weapon
    - Melee vs ranged (choice 1)
    - Sol unit upgrades your weapon
    - You also get cone attack
    - Mod can be placed such that it upgrades both attacks, can only leach from one
- Armor -- Armor
