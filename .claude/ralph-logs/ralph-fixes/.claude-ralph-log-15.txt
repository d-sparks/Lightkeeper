Done. Here's what I found and fixed:

**Two root causes of the mismatch:**

1. **Wrong direction in iso mode** — The old code used world-space angles (`Math.cos(angle)`, `Math.sin(angle)`) directly as screen-space offsets from the iso center point. But in isometric view, world "right" maps to screen "down-right". The cone visual was pointing in the wrong direction.

2. **Wrong range in iso mode** — The old code applied a uniform `* 1.5` scale to the range, but the iso transform is anisotropic (scales ~1.06x in one diagonal, ~2.12x in the other). Depending on aim direction, the visual was up to 40% too large or 29% too small.

**The fix:** Rewrote `renderConeEffects()` to sample 20 points along the cone arc in **world space**, then convert each point through `worldToIso()` — the same approach used by `renderAimLine()` and entity positioning. The visual now exactly matches the server's damage calculation.

**Range increase:** Bumped `coneRange` from 3.0 to 4.5 tiles in `abilities.json`.
