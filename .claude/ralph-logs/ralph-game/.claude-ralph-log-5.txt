**Client** (`renderer.js`): Handles the `melee_effect` event by rendering a quick slash animation:

- Expands outward from the player over 0.2 seconds
- White inner arc (4px, bright) + brown outer glow (2px, subtle) — matches the weapon color scheme
- Fades to transparent as it expires
- Supports isometric mode
