Done. Here's what I changed:

**Server** (`game-loop.js` + `scripting/actions.js`):
- Added `category` field (from item definition's `type`, e.g. `"key"`) to all item spawn points and to the state broadcast, so the client knows the item category.

**Client** (`renderer.js`):
- Both minimap modes (isometric and top-down) now check `item.category === 'key'` to render quest/key items as **larger cyan dots** (`0x00e5ff`, using `dotLarge` size) instead of the default **small yellow dots** (`0xfdd835`, `dotSmall` size).

This means the supply manifest and all other key-type items (quest items) will visually stand out on the minimap with a distinct cyan color and larger size.
