Done. The fix is a one-line change in `client/renderer.js:758`.

**Problem:** The PixiJS renderer resolution was set to `cssZoom` (the game's internal zoom factor), but this didn't account for `window.devicePixelRatio`. On mobile devices with DPR 2-3, the canvas was rendering at 1/2 to 1/3 of native physical resolution, causing all text (name tags, damage numbers, speech bubbles, door prompts) to be stretched and blurry.

**Fix:** Multiply the renderer resolution by `devicePixelRatio`, capped at 2 to avoid excessive GPU load on DPR 3 devices. This makes the canvas backing buffer match the device's physical pixel density, rendering text crisply.
