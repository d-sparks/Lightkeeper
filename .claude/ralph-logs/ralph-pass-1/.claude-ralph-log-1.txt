Done. Here's what was happening and what I fixed:

**The bug**: When you tried to place a sol component and it failed (multi-cell shapes like `cooldown_reducer`, `power_tap`, or `guardian_core` extending past the grid edge, or clicking on an occupied cell), the server returned `false` and sent **no response at all**. You'd click and nothing would happen with zero feedback.

**The fix** (3 files):

1. **`server/game-loop.js`** — `trySolGridPlace` now returns `{ ok: false, reason: '...' }` with specific messages:
   - `"Component does not fit — too close to the grid edge."` — for multi-cell shapes that would extend beyond the grid
   - `"Cell is already occupied by another component."` — when a cell is taken

2. **`server/index.js`** — The SOL_GRID_PLACE handler now sends the error reason back to the client via a `SOL_GRID` message with an `error` field.

3. **`client/main.js`** — The SOL_GRID handler shows the error in the `#sol-grid-info` element in red text, deselects the component, and fades the error color after 2.5 seconds.
