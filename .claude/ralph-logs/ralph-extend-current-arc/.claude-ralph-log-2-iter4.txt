## CONTENT_004: The Homestead — Harvesting, Crafting & Habitable Zone Life

### New Dungeons
- **homestead_plot** (22x16): Outdoor garden area with 3 plantable garden plots (tile 20), a community notice board (tile 21), a shelter with a door leading to the interior, and spawns for Neighbor Sera, Vell, and Jorrit. Garden mites spawn for the terrace watch quest.
- **homestead_interior** (12x10): Personal shelter with a Personal Log desk (tile 23) that summarizes the player's journey, and a Seed Pot (tile 22) for planting the Umbral Seed.

### Core Mechanics
- **Garden planting/harvesting**: Players plant Resin Bush Seeds in 3 garden plots. Plants mature based on activity milestones (visiting archives, completing Yun's tutorial, buying from shops, etc.) — checked on room entry. Harvesting yields Stabilizing Resin.
- **Battery crafting**: First battery at Yun's workshop uses the materials from his tutorial + 1 Stabilizing Resin. Subsequent batteries need Umbracite + Copper Coil + Stabilizing Resin.
- **Energy restoration**: Added engine support for `energy` effect on consumable items (rechargeable_battery_l1 restores 30 energy).

### Side Quests (5 total)
1. **Pest Problem**: Get Sonic Repeller (craft at Yun or buy from Mira), give to Sera. Reward: Enriched Soil.
2. **Yun's Challenge**: Craft Modified Battery Casing (Chitin Plate + Copper Coil). Reward: permanent Sol Unit upgrade.
3. **The Seed Speaks**: Plant Umbral Seed indoors, talk to Sable for dark exposure instructions (CONTENT_005 setup).
4. **Lost Cat**: Find Ember in Archives, tell Vell. Reward: 2x Ration Packs.
5. **Terrace Watch**: Volunteer for watch rotation, defeat 2 garden mites. Reward: 3x Silicon.

### Progression Path
Players arrive from CONTENT_003 with transit_pass → get homestead deed from Hollis → access homestead through gated exit in civic center → meet Sera, get seeds → plant gardens → explore city to trigger growth → harvest resin → craft first battery at Yun's workshop. Multiple quest threads point back to the dark perimeter for CONTENT_005.
