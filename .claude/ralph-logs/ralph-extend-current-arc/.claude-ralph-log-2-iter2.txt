## CONTENT_002: The Dark Perimeter & Route to the Train Station

### 8 New Dungeon Rooms
The journey from Outpost Balor to the train station now passes through a dangerous multi-room gauntlet:

1. **Perimeter Gate** — Sgt. Fenn guards the blast door, gives a Flare, requires Sol Unit
2. **Outer Ring** — Large dark area (ambient 0.06) with Dusk Crawlers and Shade Stalkers, two paths
3. **Collapsed Relay Station** (optional) — Junction box puzzle + Nest Mother mini-boss, drops Chitin Plate
4. **The Breach** — Torn wall with combat encounter, Tannis's Dog Tags on the ground
5. **The Dead Road** — Sable encounter (player choice: help or ignore), wrecked vehicles with loot
6. **Old Watchtower** (optional) — Telescope showing Lighthouse Mara flickering, Watchtower Journal lore
7. **Station Approach** — Safe zone with Unbounded graffiti, Sable companion dialogue if helped
8. **Station Junction** — Yara's quest to clear Dusk Crawler nest for Transit Pass

### New Entities
- **3 monsters**: Shade Stalker (45 HP), Nest Mother (80 HP mini-boss), Shade Stalker Alpha (60 HP)
- **3 NPCs**: Sgt. Fenn, Sable (3 location variants with dialogue states), expanded Tech Yara
- **10 items**: Flare, Relay Log, Dog Tags, Journal, Transit Pass, Night Vision Lens, Chitin Plate, etc.
- **2 sol components**: Night Vision Lens (+15% damage), Reinforced Battery (-10% cooldown)
- **4 tileset tiles**: blast_door, junction_box_b, maintenance_hatch, transit_gate

### Quests & Story
- Main quest extended with 3 steps (cross perimeter → get transit pass → board train)
- **Relay Recovery** side quest (gated by Kade's intel flag from CONTENT_001)
- **Tannis's Tags** side quest (return dog tags for Reinforced Battery reward)
- Sable encounter is a meaningful choice affecting future dialogue/trust
- Underlumen foreshadowing via Relay Log and Watchtower Journal
- Updated existing NPCs (Old Keeper, Daley, Tannis) with reactions to new items
