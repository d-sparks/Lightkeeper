## Quest: "The Array's Appetite" (umbrasite_retrieval)

**Flow:**
1. After the player has traded umbracite with MERIDIAN-7 at the train station (`traded_umbracite_meridian` flag), visiting the Array Hub shows quest-offer dialogue
2. A choice prompt lets the player accept or decline
3. Accepting sets `meridian_umbrasite_quest_active` — MERIDIAN-7's dialogue changes to give directions
4. Player travels: deep_perimeter_east → nightside_caverns → nightside_depths
5. Picking up the `refined_umbrasite` in the depths triggers narrative text and sets `found_refined_umbrasite`
6. Returning to MERIDIAN-7 with the item triggers the exchange: umbrasite removed, Umbral Amplifier given (+60 XP)

**New content:**
- **Nightside Caverns** — 25x18 frost_crypt map with shade stalkers, gloom wraiths, shadow ambushers, and a shade stalker alpha
- **Nightside Depths** — 22x20 deeper floor with heavier enemies including a Crystal Guardian guarding the refined umbrasite deposit
- **Refined Umbrasite** — rare quest item found in the depths
- **Umbral Amplifier chip** — rare sol component reward (+0.45 damage, +0.10 cooldown reduction)
- **6 MERIDIAN-7 dialogue states** — quest offer, active, return, complete, plus NPC dialogueRules
- **5 Array Hub triggers** — quest offer choice, accept, decline, turn-in mechanics

**Follow-ups** documented in `docs/NEXT_TODOS.md` including dedicated sprites, a unique boss monster, repeatable quest tiers, and environmental hazards.
