Done. Here's a summary of what was decided and implemented:

### Design Decisions (documented in `docs/progression-system.md`)

1. **Modifiers boost generators: Yes.** `energyCostReduction` modifiers adjacent to a power generator increase its regen rate (e.g. +20% efficiency → +20% regen). Other bonus types (damage, cooldown, healOnHit) don't apply to generators.

2. **Modifiers do NOT boost other modifiers.** No chaining — keeps the system simple and prevents exponential scaling.

3. **Grid component limits: Permissive.** Duplicates allowed, no ability/modifier ratio. Stacking caps prevent degenerate builds:
   - Cooldown reduction: 75% max
   - Energy cost reduction: 75% max  
   - Heal on hit: 15 HP max per ability
   - Damage multiplier: no cap (grid space is the natural limit)

### Engine Changes (`server/game-loop.js`)

- `_rebuildAbilities`: Generators now check adjacent modifiers and apply `energyCostReduction` as a regen multiplier
- `_computeModifiedAbility`: Added stacking caps before applying bonuses
- `getSolGridForClient`: Generator cells now include `boostedEnergyRegen` and modifier info for client display
