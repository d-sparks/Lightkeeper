**`docs/art-style-guide.md`** — A comprehensive sprite and tileset style guide covering:

- **Technical specs**: 16x16 native, 2x nearest-neighbor upscale, PNG with alpha, no smoothing
- **Target aesthetic**: Dark sci-fi pixel art (Caves of Qud / Cogmind tone), readable silhouettes, top-left lighting
- **Master palette**: ~40 named colors organized by family (grays, warm, cool, purple/nightside, greens, special) with hex, RGB, and usage notes
- **Per-category conventions**: Players (color-accented engineer look), monsters (silhouette-first, warm eyes), NPCs (no helmet, relaxed posture), items (diagonal weapons, centered consumables), tilesets (lower contrast than entities)
- **Zone color identity table**: Maps each of the 12 zone themes to dominant colors and mood
- **Anti-patterns**: Pure black/white fills, too many colors per sprite, off-palette colors, dark-on-dark readability issues
