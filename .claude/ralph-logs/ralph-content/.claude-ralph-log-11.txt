Done. Here's what was wrong and what I fixed:

**Root cause:** `removePlayer()` in `game-loop.js` called `questTracker.removePlayer(playerId)` every time a player left a room — including during floor transitions. This wiped all quest progress. When the player transitioned from `outpost_entrance` (where they talk to Warden Holt) to `outpost_munitions` (where they get a weapon from Voss), their quest tracker state was deleted. So even though choosing a weapon correctly set the `received_weapon` flag, the quest tracker had no record of the `get_weapon` step being active, and the quest never advanced.

**Fix (3 files):**
- `server/game-loop.js`: Removed `questTracker.removePlayer()` from `removePlayer()` so quest state persists across floor transitions
- `server/index.js`: Added `questTracker.removePlayer()` to the WebSocket disconnect handler, where cleanup actually belongs
- `server/checkpoint-api.js`: Updated stale comments that referenced the old behavior

This was actually a broader bug — it affected ALL quests across ALL floor transitions, not just the Voss weapon quest.
