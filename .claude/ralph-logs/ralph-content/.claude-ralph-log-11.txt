Done. Here's what was wrong and what I fixed:

**Root cause**: Two things overwrote the weapon ability in slot 1:
1. The `sol_unit` item in `items.json` had `"ability": { "id": "sol_beam" }` — this directly overwrote the weapon during `_rebuildAbilities()` step 2
2. Both `sol_cone` and `sol_beam` in `abilities.json` had `defaultSlot: 1` — same slot as the weapon

**Fix** (pure content/JSON changes):
- **`abilities.json`**: Changed `sol_beam` and `sol_cone` from `defaultSlot: 1` to `defaultSlot: 2`
- **`items.json`**: Removed the `"ability": { "id": "sol_beam" }` from the sol_unit item (abilities come from the grid, not the equipment)

**Result**: After getting the sol unit, players now have:
- Slot 1 (left-click): weapon ability (blaster_shot or melee_strike)
- Slot 2 (right-click): sol_cone from the grid
