**"The Disappeared Courier"** — a 3-step investigation side quest in Meridian City.

**Flow:**
1. **Archives** — Records Clerk notices courier Halden hasn't filed manifests in 4 days, asks player to investigate (triggers after `visited_meridian_station`)
2. **Station** — Calloway confirms Halden was acting suspicious and left his logbook behind, points to Market Quarter
3. **Market** — Player finds the `courier_logbook` on the ground (only visible after Calloway's clue)
4. **Archives** — Return logbook to Records Clerk, who discovers it's evidence of a smuggling operation

**Reward:** 75 total XP across steps + Shadow Conduit sol component chip
