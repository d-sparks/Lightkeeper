Done. Here's a summary of what was added:

**Two new dungeon floors** accessible from the Dayside Solar Fields:

1. **Array Synthesis Lab** (`array_synthesis_lab`) — A manufacturing facility with sealed growth chambers containing synthetic catalyst experiments. Features an Array Research Terminal NPC that reveals different information depending on what lore items you carry.

2. **Array Deep Processing** (`array_deep_processing`) — A deeper facility centered around a massive organic growth vat. Triggers progressively reveal that the Array is running "Project Autotroph" — a centuries-long program to independently synthesize the biological catalysts it currently trades for from the Ring.

**New content added:**
- **2 monsters**: Array Sentinel (ranged kiter, 65 HP) and Array Fabricator (melee, 90 HP)
- **3 lore items**: Synthesis Progress Log, Growth Vat Telemetry, and Directive 9-Sigma (the smoking gun)
- **1 key item**: Array Clearance Badge
- **1 NPC**: Array Research Terminal with 3 dialogue states (default, has synthesis log, has directive)
- **Narrative triggers** that react to umbrasite quest completion and Sable alliance flags
