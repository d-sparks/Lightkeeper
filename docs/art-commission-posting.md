# Pixel Art Commission — 14 Sprites for Indie Dungeon Crawler

## The Project

Lightkeeper is a multiplayer browser dungeon crawler set on a tidally locked sci-fi planet. Dark pixel art aesthetic — think Caves of Qud / Cogmind meets dungeon crawlers. We need 14 sprite PNGs to replace programmer-art placeholders for the game's most visible entities.

## What We Need

**14 PNG files** — 10 unique entities across 3 categories:

| # | Entity | Type | Notes |
|---|--------|------|-------|
| 1-4 | Player character (4 color variants) | Playable | Same silhouette, different accent color (blue/red/green/orange) |
| 5 | Warden Holt | NPC | Military commander, teal uniform |
| 6 | MERIDIAN-7 | NPC | AI terminal (machine, not humanoid) |
| 7 | Sol Engineer | NPC | Workshop technician, orange coveralls |
| 8-9 | Councillor Asha Denn (2 variants) | NPC | Politician in crimson robes + travel cloak variant |
| 10 | Dusk Crawler | Monster | Insectoid alien, purple/teal |
| 11 | Frostfang Hunter | Monster | Icy wolf predator, blue/white |
| 12 | Shade Stalker | Monster | Shadow humanoid, dark gray |
| 13 | Crystal Guardian | Boss | Crystalline construct, blue/white, 3-phase boss |
| 14 | Dural Voss | Boss | Armored warlord, rust/brown/red |

## Technical Specs

- **Canvas:** 16x16 pixels per frame
- **Delivery:** 64x16 PNG horizontal strip (4 frames per entity)
- **Frame layout:** idle1 | idle2 (bob) | attack | hit
- **Background:** Fully transparent (PNG alpha)
- **Outline:** 1px dark outline (darkest shade of sprite's color family, not pure black)
- **Lighting:** Top-left source (~10 o'clock)
- **Colors:** 6-10 per sprite from a provided master palette
- **Style:** Dark sci-fi pixel art. Readable at 2x (32x32) upscale. Not cute/cartoony.

## What We Provide

- **Full art commission brief** with per-entity visual descriptions, palette hex values, and silhouette direction
- **Master color palette** (40+ colors organized by family) — all sprites must use only these colors
- **Art style guide** covering conventions, anti-patterns, and category rules
- Placeholder sprites as rough shape reference (programmer art, not art direction)

## Ideal Artist

- Experience with 16x16 or similarly constrained pixel art
- Comfortable working within a strict shared palette
- Portfolio shows dark/moody pixel art (sci-fi or fantasy dungeon crawlers)
- Can deliver readable silhouettes and strong contrast at tiny scale

## Timeline & Budget

- Open to discussing rate (per-sprite or flat for the batch)
- No hard deadline — quality over speed
- This is Batch 1; if it goes well, Batch 2 has ~20 more sprites

## How to Apply
